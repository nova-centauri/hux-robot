# Minimum electronics — V1-PROOF

The active minimum is **one MCU + IMU, two encoder wheel motors, two bidirectional H-bridges, a timed manual input link, and a compatible battery/charger**. **The minimum also includes protected logic power.** Add two small leg servos and their interface/rail after pinned-leg balance works.

Start with the equipment confirmed in [parts-on-hand.md](parts-on-hand.md). There is no required Pi, CAN network, 8S pack, camera or separate pose computer. The [electronics plan](electronics.md) owns the interfaces and the power qualification. The [budget](bom.md) covers replacements. The former eight-axis minimum is [parked](archive/stair-v1/electronics-minimum.md).
