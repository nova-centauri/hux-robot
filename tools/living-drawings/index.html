Hux living drawings

Side and top views of the working model, plus the electronics we actually have a plan for. Drag the joints, request a wheel position, or play the climb. The stair is the settled 9.5 by 9.5 inch step.

Model 2026-09-21
6" × 1.25" wheel
7.5" + 7.5" tubes
24" tall · 14" wide

Side — one leg

Positive hip angle puts the knee to the rear. Knee bend brings the lower tube forward under the hip. Balance is the 92% crouch, hip over the axle, knee behind. Request a wheel position and the hip and knee follow. The red dot is the center of mass, and the dashed line is gravity down to the tread under it. Motor boxes are rough envelopes, not parts.

Top — stance and body

Overall width is 14 inches. The head is the center box. Wheels and legs sit outside it. Track is 12.75 inches with a 1.25 inch tire. The knee folds rearward.

Side — both legs on the stair

Same leg, drawn twice. The left leg is the far one and is lighter. Both hips sit on the head. Request either joint angles or wheel positions; the other follows. Play climbs the stair. The shin cannot put the center of mass over the front wheel while the rear wheel is still down, so the rear leg shoves the body forward and the rear wheel leaves as that momentum carries the mass across. The body stands up after the mass is over the front contact. The red dot is the center of mass. The red tick on it is its velocity at full speed. The scrubber slows the shove so it can be seen.

Power splitWheel and roll current stay on the pack. A step-down makes 5 / 6 / 7.4 V for logic and pose. Do not route motor current through the flight controller.

Step-down railFeeds the controller, the receiver, and the pose joints.
Wheel FOC ×2Torque mode. About 3 N·m peak at the 6 inch wheel, roughly 60–380 rpm. Drivers not chosen.
→
In-wheel motors ×2Brushless, encoder on each, motor at the hub. The hub is turned on the lathe. Not ordered.
Hip roll ×2In V1. Dynamic class, not a stepper. On the 14 inch stance the 6 kg example is about 9.5 N·m when the other wheel lifts. Driver path is PWM, CAN, or its own FOC.
+
Knee and hip swing ×4Servo, or stepper plus a belt. Still open. A spring holds about 2.2 N·m of the two-leg crouch so the motor is free to move. If they are steppers, the flight controller does not drive the coils.
ModesPARKED, TWO_WHEEL, LEFT_ONLY, RIGHT_ONLY. The pilot selects them. One-leg modes do not themselves lift a foot.
→
Pi, laterOn hand. Owns the cameras, the face display, and the eye lights. Pathfinding comes after the four modes. Not required to balance.
or
ESP32On hand. Optional thin telemetry bridge if the Pi should not own the radio link.
Cameras ×7Two on the front for stereo. One each on the back, left, right, top, and bottom. Small modules. Not bought.
→
Face displaySmall screen on the front for a few preset faces. Roughly 2.2 by 1.0 inches.
+
Eye lights ×2The eyes are sockets. An RGB shines into each orbital cavity for expression. The light is the eye, not a separate lamp.
