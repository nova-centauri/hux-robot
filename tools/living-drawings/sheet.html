Sheet 1 — V1 layout

The first 2D sheet (R23): side, front, plan and stroke of the V1 machine at the settled geometry, drawn from the same numbers the working drawings and the sandbox use. It records the 2026-09-27 direction — one step is the finish line, a brisk walk is the pace, the knee motor stays at the knee — and settles where the eight actuators sit. Everything in red is a dimension; everything in a readout is computed, not typed.

Title block — what this sheet is drawn to

Locks carried on this sheet: 9.5" × 9.5" step (R3), 6" wheel, 7.5" + 7.5" carbon tubes (R34), 24" × 14" envelope (R15 / R35), hip roll in V1 (R16), roll axes ≤ 3" (2026-09-26), the temporary RobStride set, 8S. New on this sheet: the hip band, the leg plane, the knee inboard and the swing motor outboard, the axle spacer, the pack at the top of the head (moved up 2026-09-27). Not locked by this sheet: tube OD / wall, fittings, the spring, wire ports, the head's shape — those are Sheet 2, after this one is agreed.

1 · Side elevation

Planted leg at the 92% stance with the hip over its axle; the raised leg placed on the next tread at the rear of the slot, which is where the landing is aimed (a forward error is the failure mode). Grey leg is the raised one. Motor boxes are the vendors' housing envelopes. The red cross is the lump picture's centre of mass.

2 · Front elevation

Looking from the front at the same stance. The hip band is two RS02 roll housings on their axes 3" out with the electronics between them; the head sits above it with the 8S pack at its top. Each yoke reaches out to the leg plane. The knee RS02 hangs inboard of the tubes, the hip-swing RS00 outboard. The RS05 is drawn at its real length, which is more than the tire is wide, flush with the tire's outboard face as the Sheet 2 hub places it.

3 · Plan

From above, forward up the page. Roll housings behind the swing axis with their flanges forward into the yokes; the pack (dashed) is above them at the top of the head, 1" forward. Knees fold to the rear. Dashed outline is the 8" × 7" head.

4 · Stroke

Every pose the step needs, drawn from one hip. The annulus is the leg's reach between the knee stop and full extension. The green pose lands the raised wheel in the next slot; the brown one is the rear leg at the end of the shove — the reach that decided against a five-bar leg.
