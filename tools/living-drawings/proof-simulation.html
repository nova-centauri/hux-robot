03 / What changed

Position matters as much as balance.

The first controller passed the nominal cases but only 139 of 391 parameter runs. Small center-of-mass offsets caused drift and missed endpoints. Stronger position control was tested against the same cases and thresholds.

Strong shoves, a 20 mm square obstacle and excessive delay remain separate challenges. Their failures stay in the evidence.

The hardware baseline

Two Pololu 4752 wheel motors, two DRV8874 drivers, two reduced ST3215 leg servos, Pico 2 and an LSM6DSOX IMU.

Zero onboard cameras. External trial video. $900 planning ceiling, including shipping, tax and repair reserve.
