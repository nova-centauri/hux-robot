Selected components / Proposed connections

System architecture

These block-level paths describe the plan. They are not a final schematic or evidence of a tested assembly.

Control architecture ↗
  1. Wheel commandPico 2

    3.3 V PWM, direction and watchdog/kill-gated SLEEP.

  2. Wheel power stageDRV8874

    One Pololu 4035 carrier per wheel; fused actuator power feeds the driver.

  3. Wheel actuatorPololu 4752

    Motor leads connect to driver outputs. Encoder signals return through level conversion.

Documentation / Open work

Before the harness can be released

Record actual build details and bench results in the source documents.

Open assembly details

  • Verified received-part pinouts, connector orientation and mating connector part numbers.
  • Harness identifiers, wire labels, branch routing, lengths and strain relief.
  • Conductor sizes, connector current ratings and measured fuse sizing.
  • Grounding and common signal-reference arrangement.
  • Regulator, power-cut, watchdog and regenerated-energy protection circuits.
  • ADC input limits and behavior while the controller is unpowered; servo disable on controller/watchdog failure.
