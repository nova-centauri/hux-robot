Selected components / Proposed connections

System architecture

These block-level paths describe the plan. They are not a final schematic or evidence of a tested assembly.

Control architecture ↗
  1. Wheel commandPico 2

    3.3 V PWM, direction and nSLEEP on GP10. A 500 Hz heartbeat on GP22 feeds the kill chain.

  2. Wheel power stageDRV8874

    One Pololu 4035 carrier per wheel on one board. The switched rail of the kill chain feeds the drivers.

  3. Wheel actuatorPololu 4752

    The vendor pigtail lands on the leg junction board. Motor leads pass to the driver outputs. Encoder signals return through dividers.

Documentation / Open work

Before the harness can be released

Record actual build details and bench results in the source documents.

Open assembly details

  • Verified received-part pinouts, connector orientation and mating connector part numbers.
  • Harness identifiers, wire labels, measured run lengths against the EL-06 estimates, and strain relief.
  • Conductor sizes, connector current ratings and measured fuse size.
  • Grounding and common signal-reference arrangement.
  • Regulator, kill-chain and regenerated-energy protection circuits. The leg junction board layout and mount. The Pico-end connector decision.
  • ADC input limits and behavior while the controller is unpowered.
