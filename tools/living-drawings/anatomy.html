Fit and function findings

The animation found these points when the R04 one-leg body became the two-leg robot. The R05 kit of 2026-10-14 includes corrections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7. Items 3 and 6 remain open. V1-PROOF has no powered hips, so the servo belt drive is for the next version.

  1. Hips on the centre line

    The plan puts the hips near the rear of the body. Each strut slants forward and down to the wheel, and the wheel sits below the CoM. R04 puts the hips on the centre line, and the struts slant rearward. The robot must then lean by — at 15° / 30° / 45° to balance.

    R05: put the hips at X = +30, 30 mm rearward of the body centre. The struts slant forward. The two rear angles of the lower deck move to X = +48, clear of the upper hip nut. The lean at 15° / 30° / 40° becomes —. At the 30° neutral pose, the axle is below the body centre.

  2. Keep the R04 motor carrier

    With forward struts, use the R04 motor carrier without a mirror. The axle and the gearmotor then sit 25 mm and 32 mm rearward of the strut end, toward the hip. This puts the wheel below the CoM at neutral.

    The gearmotor clears the lower strut by 2.1 mm at 40° and by 0.23 mm at 45°. R05 limits the leg to 15–40°. These are exact CAD distances from the R05 kit. Check them by hand before powered tests.

  3. Height is above the target

    R04 is — high to the Pico 2 board, upright. The V1-PROOF target is 280–310 mm. The three-deck bench stack adds approximately 40 mm. Accept this height or remove one deck.

  4. One driver position for two wheel channels

    The lower deck has one DRV8874 position, but V1-PROOF needs two. Each DRV8874 driver is 15.2 x 17.8 mm. Two drivers fit in the 36 x 24 mm component space with 3 mm between them.

    R05: put both drivers on the one driver board. Check heat and solder access on the bench.

  5. Battery in the body

    R04 has no battery position. The model mass list includes a 0.2 kg 3S pack. R05: put the pack in a tray at the bottom of the walls, along the body, between the two servo belts. The CoM and the lean on this page include the pack at this position. The pack size is an example.

  6. Circuits without a board position

    The hardware watchdog, the latching gate, the ADC conditioning and the ARM switch have no position in R04. The netlist lists these circuits as unresolved. Assign each circuit to a board before the harness release.

  7. Servos inside the body

    R04 puts the ST3215 servo outside the near wall. R05: put each servo inside the body, against its wall, below the lower deck. The output is 41 mm forward of the lower hip and 3 mm above it, at X = −11. A 3:1 belt, for example GT2 12T to 36T, drives the lower hip shaft on the inner side of the wall.

    The two belts are 45 mm apart, and the battery fits between them. The hip shaft needs a bearing in the wall. The shaft, the bearings and the belt tension are not designed.

  8. Checks that pass

    With the mirrored leg, the two encoder ends are 50 mm apart. The track is 230 mm and the overall width is 254 mm. These values agree with the V1-PROOF model. The gearmotor clears the wall bottom by 24 mm at 45°.

The CoM estimate uses the model.json mass list with assumed positions. Body items are on the body centre line. The servos are at the cradle, and the links are at their mid-points. The gearmotors and wheels are on their axes.

Measure the real CoM before you change the simulation. Record: 2026-10-14 anatomy review.
