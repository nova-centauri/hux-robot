Fit and function findings

The animation exposes these points when the R04 one-leg body becomes the two-leg robot. The R05 proposal shows the corrections, with rear hips and internal servos. The user decides each correction. The CAD and the print files did not change.

  1. Hips on the centre line

    The plan puts the hips near the rear of the body. Each strut slants forward and down to the wheel, and the wheel sits below the CoM. R04 puts the hips on the centre line of the wall, and the struts slant rearward. The robot must then lean by — at 15° / 30° / 45° to balance.

    R05 proposal: put the hips at X = +50, 10 mm from the rear edge. The struts slant forward. Mirror the motor carrier so that the gearmotor stays clear of the lower strut. Mount the two rear angles of the lower deck above the deck, clear of the upper hip nut. The lean becomes —.

  2. The JD-03 axle offset adds lean

    JD-03 puts the axle 25 mm past the strut end. With rear hips, this moves the wheel 25 mm forward of the planned position. If the axle is on the strut end, the R05 lean is —. The planned balance needs the axle on the strut end, or a lean of approximately 10° more. Decide this before the carrier R02 print.

  3. Height is above the target

    R04 is — high to the Pico 2 board, upright. The V1-PROOF target is 280–310 mm. The three-deck bench stack adds approximately 40 mm. Accept this height or remove one deck.

  4. One driver position for two wheel channels

    The lower deck has one DRV8874 position, but V1-PROOF needs two. Each DRV8874 driver is 15.2 x 17.8 mm. Two drivers fit in the 36 x 24 mm component space with 3 mm between them.

    R05 proposal: put both drivers on the one driver board. Check heat and solder access on the bench.

  5. No battery position

    R04 uses the bench supply. The model mass list includes a 0.2 kg 3S pack.

    R05 proposal: add a tray below the lower deck, at the bottom of the walls. Put the pack along the body, between the two servo belts. The pack size in the view is an example.

  6. Circuits without a board position

    The hardware watchdog, the latching gate, the ADC conditioning and the ARM switch have no position in R04. The netlist lists these circuits as unresolved. Assign each circuit to a board before the harness release.

  7. Servos inside the body

    R04 puts the ST3215 servo outside the near wall. R05 proposal: put each servo inside the body, against its wall, below the lower deck. The output is 45 mm forward of the hip and 12 mm above it. A 3:1 belt, for example GT2 12T to 36T, drives the lower hip shaft on the inner side of the wall.

    The two belts are 45 mm apart, and the battery fits between them. The hip shaft needs a bearing in the wall. The shaft, the bearings and the belt tension are not designed.

  8. Checks that pass

    With the mirrored leg, the two encoder ends are 50 mm apart. The track is 230 mm and the overall width is 254 mm. These values agree with the V1-PROOF model. The gearmotor clears the wall bottom by 24 mm at 45°.

The CoM estimate uses the model.json mass list with assumed positions. Body items are on the body centre line. The servos are at the cradle, and the links are at their mid-points. The gearmotors and wheels are on their axes.
