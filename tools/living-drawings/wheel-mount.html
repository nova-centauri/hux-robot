Mechanical / joint detail JD-03

Wheel drive mount

The Pololu 4752 gearmotor bolts to the wheel carrier and turns the Ø100 wheel on its own output shaft. This sheet gives the geometry of that mount.

JD-03 / R02

Design proposal R02, not a measured result. The dimensions come from the Pololu drawings and the Pololu STEP model. The user did not measure the received motor yet. Measure the motor and the wheel at the fit session before you print the carrier.
R05 status. R05 uses this carrier without a mirror, as part 06. With the forward struts of R05, the axle and the gearmotor sit toward the hip, below the body centre at 30°. The R05 leg limit is 40°, because the gearmotor clears the lower strut by only 0.23 mm at 45°. The side view on this sheet shows the original rearward strut. 01 / What drives the design

Three facts from the vendor data

The shaft is 7 mm off the gearbox axis

The 37D gearbox puts its output shaft 7.0 mm from the axis of the motor body. The six M3 holes sit on a Ø31 circle around the body axis, not around the shaft. The carrier must put the motor body away from the link pivots.

The shaft is short

The shaft is Ø6 and 16 mm long, with a 12 mm flat at the tip. The wheel must sit next to the carrier plate. No link and no bearing fits between the carrier and the wheel.

A parallelogram keeps every carrier point on one arc

The R01 model puts the axle on the lower carrier pivot. The lower link also ends there. The real axle moves 25 mm rearward on the carrier. The leg travel and the height do not change. Only the balance line moves.

Axle direction

Baseline: axle 25 mm rearward of the lower pivot, gearbox axis 32 mm rearward. The gearbox clears the lower link end by 5.6 mm at every angle. The floor clears the motor body by 31.6 mm.

The body height does not change. The wheel contact moves 25 mm rearward of the R01 kinematic axle. The balance trim must use the new axle.

06 / Load screen

What the shaft and the gearbox carry

Radial load

At 3.0 kg and a 60/40 split, one wheel carries 17.7 N. The wheel midplane is 22.5 mm from the gearbox face. The static moment on the shaft is 0.40 N·m. With a 3× shock allowance the bending stress in the Ø6 steel shaft is 56 MPa. The shaft is not the limit.

Gearbox support

Pololu publishes no radial load rating for the 37D output shaft. The wheel load goes through the gearbox bushing. Record the shaft play before the trials and after each trial. If the play grows, add an outboard support in the next revision.

Torque

The plan needs 0.15 N·m continuous (15 kg·mm) and 0.50 N·m short peaks (51 kg·mm). The gearbox limits are 100 kg·mm continuous and 250 kg·mm instantaneous. The maximum efficiency point of this motor is 18 kg·mm at 280 rpm.

This differs from the mechanical plan. The plan asks for a supported axle with its own bearing path. The 16 mm shaft leaves no room for an outboard bearing without a longer axle and a coupling. The proposal mounts the wheel on the motor shaft, as Pololu intends. The play measurement is the release condition for this choice.

07 / Rejected arrangements

Why the other layouts do not work

ArrangementResult
Axle on the lower carrier pivot (R01 model)The lower link bolt and the shaft want the same axis.
Motor inboard of the R01 carrier, wheel outboard of both linksThe wheel face must be 28 mm from the carrier. The shaft tip is at 22 mm.
Wheel inboard, motor outboard of the legThe shaft cannot reach through the carrier and both link planes.
Axle forward of the lower pivotThe lower link passes over the motor body. The clearance is −3.8 mm at 45°.
Shaft offset toward the motor body sideThe gearbox overlaps the lower link end by 8 mm. The body axis must sit further from the pivots than the shaft.
Axle below the lower pivotPasses the clearance checks. It raises the body 25 mm and cuts the floor clearance under the motor to 24.6 mm. Kept as the alternative.

08 / Before you print

Measure at the fit session

  1. Measure the boss diameter and length, the shaft length and the start of the flat on the received motor.
  2. Measure the distance between two neighbor mount holes. The drawing says 15.5 mm.
  3. Measure the shaft offset from the gearbox body. The drawing says 7.0 mm.
  4. Note the angle of the lead exit at the encoder end.
  5. Weigh the motor. Record the mass in the inventory.
  6. When the wheels arrive, measure the seat depth and the web width of the wheel core. Weigh one wheel and one adapter.
  7. Print the 1:1 template. Put the motor on it. Compare the holes.
  8. Record the results in the mechanical test journal under step M4.

This sheet does not establish: a printed fit, a load capacity, a bushing life, a tire traction value or a released drawing. The simulation still uses the R01 axle position. Update the model and the balance trim when the carrier revision is printed and measured.
