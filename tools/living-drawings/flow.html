Motion

Three branches leave the flight controller in parallel. Wheel current stays on the pack.

Wheel FOC ×2Torque mode. About 3 N·m peak at the 6 inch wheel, roughly 60–380 rpm. Drivers not chosen.
→
In-wheel motors ×2Brushless, encoder on each, motor at the hub. The hub is turned on the lathe. Not ordered.
Hip roll ×2In V1. Dynamic class, not a stepper. On the 14 inch stance the 6 kg example is about 9.5 N·m when the other wheel lifts. Driver path is PWM, CAN, or its own FOC.
Knee and hip swing ×4Servo, or stepper plus a belt. Still open. A spring holds about 2.2 N·m of the two-leg crouch so the motor is free to move. If they are steppers, the flight controller does not drive the coils.
