What this is and is not

  • Same plant and controller. The page loads tools/v1-proof/sim.js itself: Rapier rigid bodies at 1 kHz, encoder-quantized odometry, delayed noisy attitude, the 500 Hz discrete LQR, voltage- and speed-limited motor torque, drive lag, deadband and optional backlash.
  • Fixtures. The lanes are the physical protocol's fixtures: 3° grade, 3° cross-slope, 5 mm bump (both wheels and one wheel), 3 mm seam, plus the 20 mm threshold the study records as a failure. Fixture ramps have kinks and transitions; the study tilted the whole floor.
  • Leg height is a preview. Pinned 15°, 30° and 45° poses were in the study and pass. Moving between them re-places the CoM and pitch trim at 10°/s; leg inertia, servo torque and its reaction on the body are not modelled. Powered height motion is unvalidated.
  • Size. Body, links, wheels and motors are drawn from model.json envelopes: 120 × 110 × 150 mm body, 110 mm links, 100 mm wheels at 230 mm track. Packaging, belts and wiring are not detailed. The dashed box is the parked STAIR-V1 envelope for scale.
  • Not modelled. Raw IMU fusion, firmware timing, thermal limits, tire compliance, regeneration, servo dynamics. A good run here is a candidate for a bench test, not evidence the robot works.
