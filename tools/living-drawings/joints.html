Joint details

Three close-up studies for the current parallel-link leg. Each side has one powered leg coordinate. Both links connect the body to the same wheel carrier.

JD / V1-PROOF

Drawing status. JD-01 and JD-02 are schematic placeholders. JD-03 is a design proposal with exact vendor dimensions. Hardware sizes, mount positions, bearing fits and load capacity await measurements and review. The R05 assembly guide is the current assembly reference.
R05 status. R05 uses the same 110 mm struts, the 40 mm hip spacing, the 2 mm and 12 mm spacers and the JD-03 carrier. In R05 the hips are at the rear, the struts slant forward, and the leg limit is 40°. These sheets show the original rearward slant. The dimensions of each joint do not change.

Design proposal R02

Wheel carrier and axle

The Pololu 4752 gearmotor bolts to the carrier. The wheel sits on the motor shaft, outboard of the carrier. The axle moves 25 mm rearward of the lower pivot. The lower link ends on that pivot, and the 37D shaft is 7 mm off the motor axis.

Still needed: measurements of the received motor, the wheel seat depth, a printed R02 carrier, a fit check and the shaft play record. The wheel drive mount sheet has the live geometry, the hole table and the load screen.
