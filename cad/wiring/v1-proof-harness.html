Assembly method Prepare · crimp · insert · check

Tools: wire cutter, wire stripper, contact-compatible crimp tool, multimeter, labels, heat-shrink tubing and a ruler.

  1. Disconnect the battery, USB cable and bench supply. Disconnect the cable from the boards.
  2. Confirm the connector parts, position 1 and each open item on the selected card.
  3. Route a loose wire on the frame. Allow room at each connector for strain relief.
  4. Record the required length. Cut the wire after this check.
  5. Strip the wire to the contact specification. Do not cut the copper strands.
  6. Crimp a practice contact with the correct die. Check the conductor grip and insulation support.
  7. Crimp one contact onto each specified wire end. Inspect each crimp.
  8. Insert each contact from the wire-entry side into its assigned position. Check retention with a light pull.
  9. Leave each EMPTY position without a contact. Label both ends with their full connector IDs.
  10. Complete the continuity and short checks on the selected card.

Pre-crimped XH leads are an alternative. Verify their contact, wire size, insulation and length before use.

The number in AWG identifies wire size. Smaller AWG numbers mean thicker wire. Check both conductor size and insulation diameter.

H6 is an assembly ID. H037 is a wire ID. B12 is a bundle ID. EL-01 uses separate W-series drawing IDs.

Full endpoint labels remove ambiguity: DB.JML.1 means driver board, connector JML, position 1.
