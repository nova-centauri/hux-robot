**Gate:** several **2D sketch layouts** (side / front / top + linkage) must exist **before** Blender or other 3D CAD (R23). A `.blend` with no preceding 2D is a process miss. **Status 2026-09-27:** Sheet 1 (layout, [`sheet.html`](../tools/living-drawings/sheet.html)) and Sheet 2 (tubes, fittings, spring, hub, wires, [`sheet2.html`](../tools/living-drawings/sheet2.html)) exist; the leg and the hip band come here once Steve agrees both.

V1 expected: one wheel-leg — **carbon-tube spars** + printed / machined **end fittings**, sized toward a ~9.5" step inside **~24" tall / ~14" wide** (see [`../docs/mechanical.md`](../docs/mechanical.md) and [`../docs/checklists/mechanical-v1.md`](../docs/checklists/mechanical-v1.md)).
